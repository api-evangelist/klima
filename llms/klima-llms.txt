# Klima (Climate Labs GmbH)

> Klima was a Berlin-based consumer climate-action company whose mobile app let individuals calculate
> a personal carbon footprint and offset it via a subscription funding Gold Standard and Verified
> Carbon Standard projects. Founded 2019 by Markus Gilles, Jonas Brandau and Andreas Pursian-Ehrlich;
> backed by HV Capital, Keen Venture Partners, Headline, 468 Capital and Adjacent. Acquired by Wren
> on 2025-04-30. Klima publishes no public API, developer portal, SDKs or machine-readable specs;
> klima.com is now a single-page wind-down notice.
